## Scope and method

Both implementations; mixed, tiny-many and large-few; 1024 MiB/32 transfers and 2048 MiB/64 transfers; cold-create, unchanged-update, changed-update and pruned-update; five repetitions per cell. Each run has 240 phase samples. Provider metrics come from CloudWatch REPORT; providerSummary and its phase timings are explanatory internal telemetry.

47 comparison identity checks passed, including both measured commits, configuration/workload identity, test-account/context hashes, upstream provider code and SDK/CDK tool identities. The script also requires complete cleanup records and manifest-bound unmodified ledgers. Independent cleanup confirmed all 60 stacks and 60 captured buckets were absent for each run.

Statistics use the repository's summarize helper: median, interpolated Q1/Q3, IQR and min/max. Deltas are after minus before; positive duration/billing/memory deltas are worse. IQR overlap is descriptive, not a significance test. No samples are dropped. Repetition numbers do not make separate before/after executions statistically paired.

No numerical acceptance threshold was prespecified for this maintenance refresh. The decision retains the reviewed correctness benefits with the measured regression tradeoff; it does not claim a speedup or zero regression. Assess every cell against its spread and upstream controls rather than averaging away slower cells.

## Interpretation limits and follow-up

- Five samples per cell give descriptive spread, not a strong significance claim. Missing optional init measurements remain unavailable; warm invocations are never filled with zero.
- Compare cold-create separately from unchanged updates. Init duration covers observed Lambda initialization, not a directly instrumented SDK startup cost.
- Summed concurrent task timings can exceed transfer wall time; medians of sub-phases are not additive. No residual is labelled CPU time.
- Source wait/refetch counters alone do not establish S3 throttling. Check source getRetries/getErrors and destination retryAttempts/throttledAttempts, plus fetched bytes, high-water memory, reader counts and callback attempts.
- Max memory is the phase-local CloudWatch high-water mark, not average RSS. Local/CDK wall times are secondary observations from the same concurrent topology, not sequential latency controls.
- The run provider architecture bounds the conclusion; the arm64 Shin matrix establishes no x86 provider performance result. The profiles do not isolate CRC from compression safety assertions, signing state, callback changes or other bundled dependency changes.
- Complete validated ledgers do not prove deployed CloudFormation replacement correctness. That verification remains separate from this measured maintenance decision.

The [latest provider telemetry](ci-telemetry.md) reports the AFTER run. Both complete sample sets, including all original provider summaries, remain in [results.jsonl](results.jsonl), with their matching identities in [runs.jsonl](runs.jsonl). The [benchmark page](../docs/benchmark.md#runtime-dependency-maintenance-decision) explains the main phase-level changes and the decision.
